How to learn them

  • Start with the natural notes on the two lowest strings — they are the roots of most chord shapes.
  • Remember the two pairs with no sharp between them: E–F and B–C.
  • Drill a few minutes a day. Fretquency asks one position at a time and brings back the ones you miss.
